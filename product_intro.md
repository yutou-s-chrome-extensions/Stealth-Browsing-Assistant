# Product Introduction - Stealth Browsing Assistant

## English

**Stealth Browsing Assistant** is a Chrome extension designed to enhance your online privacy. It allows you to visit specific websites without leaving traces in your regular browser history. The extension also attempts to reduce (but may not completely eliminate) address bar suggestions for these sites. All activity for these designated sites is instead recorded in a separate, private log that only you can access within the extension.

**Core Features:**

*   **Ignore List Management:** Easily add or remove websites (domains) to your personal "ignore list" through the extension's settings page.
*   **Automatic History Filtering:** Once a site is on your ignore list, visits to it will not appear in Chrome's standard history.
*   **Reduced Address Bar Suggestions:** The extension actively works to minimize address bar suggestions for ignored sites, though due to Chrome's internal caching mechanisms, some suggestions may still appear occasionally.
*   **Private Browsing Log:** Access a dedicated log within the extension that records your visits to ignored sites, including the URL, page title, and timestamp.
*   **Quick Toggle via Popup:** Use the extension's popup to quickly see if the current website is on your ignore list and add or remove it with a single click.
*   **Multi-language Support:** The interface is available in English and Simplified Chinese.

**Why Use Stealth Browsing Assistant?**

Whether you're researching sensitive topics, managing accounts on shared devices, or simply prefer to keep certain browsing activities private, Stealth Browsing Assistant offers a straightforward solution to maintain your confidentiality without needing to constantly switch to incognito mode for specific sites.

**Note on Address Bar Suggestions:** Due to Chrome's internal mechanisms, address bar suggestions might still occasionally appear for ignored sites, especially after frequent visits. The extension employs multiple strategies to minimize this, including periodic cleaning of history entries related to ignored domains.

## 中文 (Simplified Chinese)

**隐秘浏览助手** 是一款旨在增强您在线隐私的 Chrome 扩展程序。它允许您访问特定网站，而不会在常规浏览器历史记录中留下痕迹。此扩展程序还尝试减少（但可能无法完全消除）这些网站在地址栏中的联想建议。这些指定网站的所有活动都会被记录在一个独立的、只有您才能在扩展程序内部访问的私密日志中。

**核心功能：**

*   **忽略名单管理：** 通过扩展程序的设置页面，轻松地将网站（域名）添加或移出您的个人"忽略名单"。
*   **自动历史记录过滤：** 一旦某个网站在您的忽略名单上，对该网站的访问将不会出现在 Chrome 的标准历史记录中。
*   **减少地址栏联想建议：** 扩展程序积极工作以最小化忽略网站的地址栏联想建议，但由于 Chrome 的内部缓存机制，某些建议可能仍会偶尔出现。
*   **隐私浏览日志：** 在扩展程序内访问一个专门的日志，该日志记录您对已忽略网站的访问，包括 URL、页面标题和时间戳。
*   **通过弹出窗口快速切换：** 使用扩展程序的弹出窗口，快速查看当前网站是否在您的忽略名单上，并一键添加或移除它。
*   **多语言支持：** 界面支持英文和简体中文。

**为什么要使用隐秘浏览助手？**

无论您是在研究敏感话题、在共享设备上管理帐户，还是仅仅希望对某些浏览活动保密，隐秘浏览助手都提供了一个简单的解决方案，帮助您维护隐私，而无需针对特定网站频繁切换到无痕模式。

**关于地址栏联想建议的说明：** 由于 Chrome 的内部机制，被忽略网站的地址栏联想建议仍可能偶尔出现，尤其是在频繁访问后。扩展程序采用多种策略来最小化这种情况，包括定期清理与被忽略域名相关的历史记录条目。
