# Privacy Policy - Stealth Browsing Assistant

## English

**Effective Date:** 2024-07-26

Thank you for using Stealth Browsing Assistant (the "Extension"). This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled by the Extension.

**1. No Data Collection or Transmission**

The Stealth Browsing Assistant is designed with your privacy as a priority. The Extension **does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data or browsing activity to any external servers or third parties.**

**2. Local Data Storage**

All data managed by the Extension is stored locally on your computer using Chrome's `chrome.storage.local` API. This data includes:

*   **Ignore List:** The list of website domains that you designate to be ignored by your standard browser history.
*   **Private Browsing Log:** A log of your visits (URL, page title, timestamp) to the websites on your Ignore List. This log is only created if a site is on the Ignore List and is stored locally.

This locally stored data is not synchronized with your Google Account or any other cloud service by the Extension itself. It remains confined to the browser profile where the Extension is installed.

**3. Data Control**

You have full control over the data stored by the Extension:

*   You can add or remove websites from your Ignore List at any time through the Extension's options page.
*   You can view your Private Browsing Log at any time through the Extension's options page.
*   You can delete individual entries from your Private Browsing Log or clear the entire log at any time through the Extension's options page.
*   Uninstalling the Extension will typically remove all associated locally stored data, subject to Chrome's standard uninstall procedures.

**4. Permissions**

The Extension requires the following permissions to function, as declared in its manifest file:

*   `storage`: To store your Ignore List and Private Browsing Log locally.
*   `history`: To delete browsing history entries for websites on your Ignore List from Chrome's standard history.
*   `webNavigation`: To detect when you visit a website so it can check against your Ignore List.
*   `tabs`: To identify the current website's URL for the "quick add/remove" feature in the popup.

These permissions are used solely for the core functionality of the Extension as described.

**5. Changes to This Privacy Policy**

We may update this Privacy Policy in the future if the Extension's functionality changes. Any updates will be reflected with a new effective date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

**6. Contact Us**

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please refer to the support information provided on the Chrome Web Store page for this extension.

## 中文 (Simplified Chinese)

**生效日期：** 2024年7月26日

感谢您使用隐秘浏览助手（"本扩展程序"）。本隐私政策描述了本扩展程序如何处理您的信息。

**1. 无数据收集或传输**

隐秘浏览助手的设计将您的隐私放在首位。本扩展程序**不会收集、存储或将您的任何个人数据或浏览活动传输到任何外部服务器或第三方。**

**2. 本地数据存储**

本扩展程序管理的所有数据都使用 Chrome 的 `chrome.storage.local` API 存储在您的计算机本地。这些数据包括：

*   **忽略名单：** 您指定希望从标准浏览器历史记录中忽略的网站域名列表。
*   **隐私浏览日志：** 您访问忽略名单上网站的日志（URL、页面标题、时间戳）。只有当网站在忽略名单上时才会创建此日志，并且该日志存储在本地。

这些本地存储的数据不会被本扩展程序同步到您的 Google 帐户或任何其他云服务。它仅限于安装了本扩展程序的浏览器配置文件中。

**3. 数据控制权**

您对本扩展程序存储的数据拥有完全控制权：

*   您可以随时通过本扩展程序的选项页面从您的忽略名单中添加或删除网站。
*   您可以随时通过本扩展程序的选项页面查看您的隐私浏览日志。
*   您可以随时通过本扩展程序的选项页面从您的隐私浏览日志中删除单个条目或清除整个日志。
*   卸载本扩展程序通常会删除所有相关的本地存储数据，具体取决于 Chrome 的标准卸载程序。

**4. 权限**

本扩展程序需要以下权限才能运行，这些权限已在其清单文件中声明：

*   `storage`：用于在本地存储您的忽略名单和隐私浏览日志。
*   `history`：用于从 Chrome 的标准历史记录中删除忽略名单上网站的浏览历史条目。
*   `webNavigation`：用于检测您何时访问网站，以便对照您的忽略名单进行检查。
*   `tabs`：用于在弹出窗口的"快速添加/删除"功能中识别当前网站的 URL。

这些权限仅用于如上所述的本扩展程序的核心功能。

**5. 本隐私政策的变更**

如果本扩展程序的功能发生变化，我们将来可能会更新本隐私政策。任何更新都将反映新的生效日期。我们鼓励您定期查看本隐私政策。

**6. 联系我们**

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请参阅此扩展程序在 Chrome 应用商店页面上提供的支持信息。
